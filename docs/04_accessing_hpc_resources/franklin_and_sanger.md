# Franklin and Sanger

Franklin and Sanger are compute servers that form part of the NHM's molecular biology computing facility. They are used mainly for phylogenetics, sequence database searching, and molecular sequence analysis, and are set up to be flexible and interactive. Unlike the NHM HPC and Crop Diversity, these servers do not use a job scheduler (i.e. SLURM) — this means you can run commands directly in the terminal without needing to write and submit a job script. This makes them well suited for interactive work, testing commands, or analyses that do not require large amounts of CPU or memory.

As these servers are shared between users and have no job scheduler to manage resources, please be mindful of the computational load you place on them. Running very large or long jobs can affect other users. For intensive analyses, the NHM HPC is more appropriate.
